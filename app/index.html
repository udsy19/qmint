QMint is an open-source research platform. Results are simulations, published analytic values or committed datasets, each labelled with its evidence kind. It is not money, not a payment service and not a deployable quantum currency.

  • MintPrepare and verify a BB84 token
  • AttackCounterfeiting and double-verification
  • BenchmarksQuantum vs classical under matched assumptions
  • CertificatesSecurity certificate studio
  • ResearchPapers, theorems, obligations, datasets
  • DocsDeveloper documentation
