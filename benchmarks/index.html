Read before comparing

No single 'better' score exists. Lane H's X11 matrix marks each classical construction QUALIFIED (comparable only after pricing the listed concessions) or INCOMPARABLE (fails the double-spend goal, or uses a different trust model) against the ideal S1 model; no pair is COMPARABLE. Classical latencies are MODELLED lower bounds on SYNTHETIC light-cone networks; S1 is information-theoretic in an ideal model.

Runs are overdispersed (χ²/dof = 10.5): per-run Clopper–Pearson intervals assume i.i.d. trials, so the pooled hull is the conservative interval. This is run-to-run heterogeneity, not temporal drift: a run-level Welch test of first vs second half gives p = 0.59 (no evidence of drift). Calibration record schiansky2023-zenodo7979319-Z-v1.
