01The problem

01. Copying is free. Spending twice is the attack.

Digital value is a string of bits, and bits copy perfectly. Classical payment systems don't stop the copy. They make the second spend fail, and the way they do that decides whom you have to trust.

Where classical systems put the trust

  1. H1Online ledgerEvery payment waits for a coordinator round trip, and the coordinator sees where you pay.
  2. H5Secure hardwareSpending twice is blocked only while the tamper-resistance assumption holds.
  3. H6Offline e-cashBoth merchants accept. The double spender is identified later, at deposit.
  4. Classical comparators from lane H (qmint/classical). Section 04 matches them against S-money.
02The quantum primitive

02. A note made of states nobody can read without disturbing them.

In Wiesner's scheme the bank prepares each qubit in one of four states and keeps the choice secret. To verify, it measures each qubit in the basis it chose. An unknown quantum state cannot be copied exactly, and a measurement in the wrong basis randomises the answer.

What no-cloning alone does not give you

  1. a.

    Imperfect copies still pass sometimes.

    The best counterfeiting channel passes both checks with probability 3/4 per qubit. Security is the exponent, (3/4)ⁿ, which reaches 10⁻⁹ only at n = 73.

    THEOREM: Bound transcribed from a peer-reviewed proof
  2. b.

    The secret is the security.

    If the bank's bases and bits leak, a forger prepares two genuine notes and passes both checks with probability 1. QMint runs this as a control (CTRL_secret_leak).

    OUT-OF-MODEL: Request violates the proof's trust/physical model
  3. c.

    Real channels lose and flip photons.

    A verifier that tolerates errors and loss plays a different game (W2), with no closed-form bound here. Against a toy verifier that allows half the qubits to be lost, an attack that splits the note and reports losses wins every time.

    MONTE-CARLO (lower bound): Sampled attack success: a LOWER bound on worst-case attack success, never an upper bound
  4. d.

    A simulator copies freely.

    A simulated state vector is classical data. Nothing on this site demonstrates physical no-cloning; it computes what the model predicts.

    SIMULATOR-IDEAL: Noiseless circuit/statevector simulation

04Quantum vs classical

04. Under matched assumptions, the difference is the adversary model, not speed.

S-money is not coordination-free. Before presentation, the bit c = b ⊕ z must be relayed to both sites. A classical late-commit escrow with private commitment has the same causal structure and the same latency.

What remains is what each side assumes. The ideal S-money bound (1/2 + 1/(2√2))ᴺ is information-theoretic. The classical constructions that match it rely on hardness assumptions or trusted setup. A claim that quantum tokens are faster needs a scenario where those assumptions are ruled out.

05Proof, not hype

05. Three kinds of evidence, never blended.

A theorem bounds every adversary. A numerical result is only as good as its certificate. A measurement describes one apparatus. QMint labels each number with which one it is.
