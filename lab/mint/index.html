Lab / Mint

Quantum money laboratory

Mint a Wiesner quantum note, pass it through a noise channel, and let the bank verify it. Every number says whether it is exact, sampled or simulated, and where it came from.

Research simulation on a local simulator. Not money, not a payment service, and nothing here runs on quantum hardware.
