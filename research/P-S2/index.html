Assumptionsassumptions
  • –Table 6 A: each qubit-labelled state is prepared from exactly two orthonormal qubit bases.
  • –Table 6 B: t and u are generated with exactly product (independent per-position) distributions with biases beta_PS, beta_PB.
  • –Table 6 C/D: the reported set Lambda gives Bob no information on Alice's bases W and bit z (follows from E,F with reporting strategy 1/2, Lemma 5).
  • –Table 6 E: Bob cannot use undeclared degrees of freedom to affect/learn Alice's detectors (side channels excluded).
  • –Table 6 F: Alice's detectors have equal efficiency; dark-count products equal; dark counts and detections independent.
  • –Table 6 G: from Alice's view Bob's pulses are Fock-state mixtures (phase-randomised / low-coherence source).
  • –Table 5 item 1: multiphoton probability P_noqub > 0 is known (upper bounded); Table 5 item 2: basis misalignment angle theta in (0, pi/4).
  • –Lemmas 2,3 use the Chernoff bounds of SI Proposition 1, which require INDEPENDENT Bernoulli rounds (detections, errors).
  • –Unforgeability: Bob honest, Alice arbitrary; robustness/correctness: both honest; privacy: Alice honest, Bob arbitrary (under C-F).

Mathematical boundmathematical_bound

eps_rob = exp(-(P_det N/2)(1 - gamma_det/P_det)^2) [Eq. 3, domain Eq. 2] eps_cor = exp(-(P_det(1-2b_PB)N/4)(1 - 2 nu_cor/(P_det(1-2b_PB)))^2) + exp(-(E nu_cor N/3)(gamma_err/E - 1)^2) [Eq. 5, domain Eq. 4] eps_priv = beta_E [Eq. 6] eps_unf = exp(-(P_noqub N/3)(nu_unf/P_noqub - 1)^2) + exp(-N f) [Eq. 10, domain Eq. 7 and f > 0] f = (g_det - nu_unf)[lambda/2 (1 - delta/lambda)^2 - ln(1 + 2 b_PS)] - (1 - (g_det - nu_unf)) ln(1 + h) [Eq. 8] h = 2 b_PS sqrt(1/2 + 2 b_PB^2 + (1/2 - 2 b_PB^2) sin(2 theta)), delta = g_det g_err/(g_det - nu_unf) [Eq. 9] lambda = (1/2)(1 - sqrt(1 - [1 - O(theta)^2](1 - 4 b_PB^2))), O(theta) = (cos theta + sin theta)/sqrt 2 [Table 5 item 3, item 2] 2^M points (Eq. 11): rob^M = M rob, cor^M = M cor, priv^M = 2^-M[(1 + 2 priv)^M - 1], unf^M = C unf, C = L(L-1)/2

Caveatscaveats
  • –Out-of-domain parameters (any constraint of Eqs. 2,4,7 violated, or f <= 0) return NO value, never an extrapolated exponential.
  • –The paper's own experimental tuple (beta_PB=2.4e-3, beta_PS=3.6e-3, theta<=10deg, E=0.058) does NOT satisfy Theorem 1 (f <= 0): unforgeability is not certified by the paper for its experiment, as the paper itself states.
  • –nu_cor, nu_unf, gamma_err are analysis/protocol choices; any admissible choice gives a valid bound.
  • –Assumptions A,B,E,F,G are physical idealisations that cannot be verified from counts; they must be declared and are flagged as such.
