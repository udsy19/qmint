Game definitiongame_definition

Ideal two-presentation-point scheme (M=1). IQT1 (Table 1): B sends N BB84 states |phi_{t_k u_k}>. A measures each in random basis y_k, keeps outcomes x, sends d = y (z=0) or the bitwise complement of y (z=1) to B. In stage II, A chooses b, sends c = b xor z. B_i computes d~_i, and B_b validates x if x_b = t_b on Delta_b = {k : d~_{b,k} = u_k}. IQT2: A measures all qubits in one basis z. Dishonest Alice wins if she gets valid tokens at BOTH Q0 and Q1 (SI p.5: a_0 = t_0 and b_1 = t_1). Proof: Lemma 6 with gamma_err=0, beta_PB=0, theta=0 (O = 1/sqrt2), so lambda = 1/2 - 1/(2 sqrt2) and max ||D_{a,b}|| <= (1-lambda)^N.

Assumptionsassumptions
  • –no losses, no errors, exact BB84 states, uniform bases/states (beta_PB = beta_PS = 0)
  • –secure and authenticated classical channels among agents of the same party; secure labs
  • –Bob honest (unforgeability is against dishonest Alice)
Quantifier over adversariesquantifier_over_adversaries

general cheating strategy: joint projective measurement on the N qubits plus an arbitrary finite-dimensional ancilla (SI p.5)
